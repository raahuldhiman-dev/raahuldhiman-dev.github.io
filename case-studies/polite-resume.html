01 — The Problem

Why Job Seekers Fail the ATS Filter

Most resume builders sell beautiful PDFs that are invisible to Applicant Tracking Systems. Job seekers spend hours customizing a document that gets rejected before a human ever reads it.

🚬

ATS Rejection at Scale

Up to 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS software before reaching a recruiter. Most resume tools optimize for visual design, not machine-readability, leaving candidates invisible to the systems that actually filter them.

📄

Blank Page Problem

Even motivated job seekers freeze when writing about themselves. Generic advice like "use action verbs" doesn't help someone who doesn't know what a recruiter actually wants to read for their specific role.

📈

No Feedback Loop

Traditional resume tools give no signal about whether the output will pass keyword matching. Users submit and hope. There's no way to know if the document is performing until interviews fail to materialize.

03 — Features

What the Platform Delivers

Seven capabilities that move a job seeker from blank page to submitted application.

📄

40+ ATS-Optimized Templates

Resume templates designed to pass keyword extraction by major ATS systems (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever). Clean formatting, no tables or columns that confuse parsers, and standard section headings that ATS systems recognize.

✍

Prewritten Role Content

Recruiter-approved bullet points and summaries for 50+ job roles, from software engineers to marketing managers. Users select bullets that match their experience and customize rather than writing from scratch.

📊

ATS Score Checker

Paste a job description and resume text to get a keyword match score. Shows which keywords from the job description are present in the resume and which are missing, before the application is submitted.

💌

Cover Letter Builder

Role-matched cover letter templates with customizable opening paragraphs, body sections, and closings. Follows the same keyword-first philosophy as the resume templates.

🔒

Membership + Paywall

Free tier gives access to template previews. Premium membership unlocks the full library, prewritten content, and ATS checker. MemberPress enforces access rules at the page and content block level.

🔍

SEO Content Strategy

Role-specific landing pages and blog content targeting high-intent search queries like "software engineer resume template ATS" and "marketing manager cover letter examples." Organic acquisition without paid ads.

📥

Instant Download

Premium members download templates as DOCX files, editable in any word processor. No proprietary builder lock-in. The user owns the file and can customize it freely outside the platform.

04 — Stack

Technology Choices

WordPress chosen for SEO velocity and ecosystem; each plugin chosen for a specific capability gap, not convenience.

Layer Technology Why This Choice
CMS WordPress Largest content ecosystem and strongest SEO tooling. The plugin market covers every required capability (membership, payments, email) without custom backend development. Time-to-launch mattered.
Page Builder Elementor Pro Enables high-fidelity template preview pages with dynamic content widgets. The theme builder powers role-specific landing pages at scale without duplicating HTML.
Membership MemberPress Access rules at the page, post, and content block level. Integrates with WooCommerce for one-time and subscription purchases. Provides member dashboards without custom development.
Payments WooCommerce + Stripe WooCommerce handles the product and checkout flow. Stripe processes payments with strong fraud controls. PayPal added as an alternative gateway for users without cards.
SEO Rank Math Pro Schema markup for template pages (HowTo, FAQPage), on-page optimization guidance, and XML sitemap management. Schema improves CTR from search results beyond raw ranking.
Email Brevo (Sendinblue) Transactional email (membership confirmation, password reset) and marketing sequences. SMTP delivery avoids shared hosting spam reputation issues.
CDN / Security Cloudflare Global CDN for asset delivery, DDoS protection, and bot mitigation. Free tier covers the traffic level of a growing SaaS without infrastructure cost.
05 — Hard Problems

What Was Actually Difficult

Three non-obvious problems that shaped how the platform was built.

🆔 ATS Parser Compatibility Across Template Designs

ATS systems parse resumes differently. A template that looks clean to a human can produce garbage output when parsed by Workday's or Greenhouse's extractors. Tables, text boxes, headers and footers, and multi-column layouts all break differently across parsers. Every template had to be validated against the parse output of the major ATS systems, not just visually reviewed. Templates with known parse failures were removed or redesigned regardless of visual quality.

✓ ATS parse validation for every template

🔒 Paywall Enforcement Without a Custom Backend

MemberPress enforces access at the WordPress page level, but template download files (DOCX, PDF) are static assets stored in the media library with publicly guessable URLs. A member who downloads a file can share the direct URL, bypassing the paywall entirely. The fix was moving premium files outside the web root and serving them through a PHP passthrough that validates the active membership session before streaming the file. No file is ever accessible via a direct URL.

✓ PHP passthrough with session validation

🔎 SEO at Scale Without Keyword Cannibalization

Role-specific resume template pages are structurally similar. Without a deliberate content strategy, Google treats them as near-duplicate content and suppresses the whole set. Each landing page required a unique angle: different primary keyword, different prewritten content examples, different FAQ schema, and unique internal linking targets. Rank Math's content analysis was used to enforce minimum uniqueness thresholds before publishing a new role page.

✓ Per-page uniqueness enforcement + FAQ schema
06 — Design Decisions

Four Calls That Defined the Product

The choices behind the template strategy, paywall model, and content approach.

01

DOCX download over in-browser editor

Many resume SaaS products lock users into a proprietary editor and charge to export. Polite Resume takes the opposite position: download a standard DOCX file and edit it in Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice. This removes friction from the first purchase decision (no "try before you buy" flow to build), and it means the product promise is fulfilled the moment the file downloads, not dependent on the quality of a custom editor.

02

WordPress over a custom-built stack

A custom Next.js + backend stack would take months to reach feature parity with what WordPress provides out of the box: SEO tooling, membership plugins, payment gateways, email, and content management. For a content-heavy SaaS where organic SEO is the primary acquisition channel, WordPress ships the product faster, with better SEO defaults from day one. The trade-off is less control over performance and no custom user dashboard without plugin overhead.

03

Role-specific landing pages over a template gallery

A generic "resume templates" gallery competes with hundreds of existing sites on broad keywords. Role-specific pages ("software engineer resume template" or "nurse resume template") target high-intent, lower-competition queries where the visitor already knows exactly what they need. Each page doubles as a sales page and an SEO target. The result is a long-tail keyword strategy that compounds over time without paid acquisition.

04

Prewritten content as the core value proposition

Most resume tools sell the template. Polite Resume sells the words inside it. Prewritten bullet points eliminate the blank-page problem that blocks most job seekers from finishing a resume. This is also more defensible than template design: anyone can copy a layout, but a library of recruiter-validated content for 50+ job roles takes significant curation effort to replicate.
